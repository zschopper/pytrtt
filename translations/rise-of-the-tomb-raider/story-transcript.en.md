## Editorial scope

The original English game export is the primary text source. A no-commentary walkthrough SRT establishes the heard
order. A public fan transcript is used only as secondary evidence for speaker attribution and scene boundaries.
Combat taunts, repeated alerts, incidental enemy chatter, merchants, and unrelated ambient dialogue are excluded.
Partial automatic captions may anchor a complete original game line when the scene and surrounding order agree.
